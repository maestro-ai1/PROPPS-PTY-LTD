- New-notes category: three new H2 sections (prop Australian money versus a real banknote, prop money AU pricing and delivery, realistic prop money) with RBA, legislation.gov.au and Australia Post outbound links.

- Four posts from the earlier pass cover the prop dollar, prop cash, realistic, filming and monetary toys terms.
- Refund policy with 3 to 8 business day refunds (a trust signal the competitors do not publish).

## Honest limits
- Neither competitor has a transactional long tail to steal. Every buying phrase they rank for is 20 searches a month or fewer and is already mapped.
- Their biggest keyword group (counterfeit, fake money detection, KD 28 to 65) is the wrong audience and a brand-safety risk. Excluded on purpose.
- Copy alone will not displace a #1 on the head terms. The remaining levers are inbound links (guest posts with art departments, film schools, prop-master directories, a Google Business Profile for Eltham) and indexing.
