# Money gun (cash cannon) product plan, 2026-11-13

Goal: unlock the largest easy pool left (money gun, 1,000 searches a month, KD 28, commercial intent, AU) under the golden keyword rule. Plan only: nothing built yet.

## 1. The product
**Party Cash Cannon Kit** (corrected 2026-11-13): a money gun (cash cannon) sold with (a) a pack of CLEARLY PRETEND play notes, the only notes that are loaded or fired (brightly coloured or oversized, printed PLAY MONEY, not a reproduction of an Australian note), and (b) one stack of specimen-marked PROPPS notes packed separately for the photo or film shot, kept by the host and never fired. Category: `/shop/event-and-party-props/`. One product, one URL, one primary.

Correction: the first version of this plan loaded realistic specimen-marked notes into the gun. That contradicts our own party-money guide (realistic notes must not be scattered) and the s22 position, so it was dropped.

Why a kit and not a bare gun: the site minimum order is $300 AUD, so a $40 to $90 gun alone cannot be bought. A kit (gun + notes) priced to reach the minimum, or sold as an add-on to the bulk pack, solves it.

## 2. Keywords (golden rule)
Data we hold (Semrush AU): money gun C 1,000 / KD 28. Autocomplete seeds seen: prop money gun, toy money gun, play money gun, fake money for a money gun, prop money for money gun, custom fake money for money gun.
Step 0 before any build: Semrush AU Keyword Overview, Update metrics, wait for Now, on money gun, cash cannon, money cannon, money shooter, money gun australia, buy money gun, party money gun, money gun for sale, plus the autocomplete seeds above.
Mapping rule: primary = money gun (C, 1,000/28) on the product only; 5 secondaries = the highest-volume T/C terms from the pull at KD 28 or below; anything under about 50 searches goes to the FAQ or blog. Nothing may duplicate a keyword already mapped (checked against the map, tags, ledger). The category primary (play money australia) does not change. `money gun` moves from the hold list to mapped, and the ledger and the summary page are rebuilt.

## 4. Compliance (non-negotiable)
- Crimes (Currency) Act 1981 s22: the notes loaded are the existing non-legal-tender, specimen-marked reproduction props. No copy such as "make it rain real cash", "spendable" or "looks real enough to spend". Positioned for parties, photo and video shoots, stage and film effects.
- Product safety: a battery-powered fan device with flying paper. Supplier must provide safety or conformity information; label an age guide; no use on or near people's faces.
- Shipping: if the gun is battery powered, lithium batteries have Australia Post restrictions. Confirm with Australia Post before listing; this can decide whether we sell battery or mains/USB-cable models.

## 5. Honest expectations
- 1,000 searches a month is the whole market for the head term. A first-page result might bring 50 to 100 visits a month, and 1 to 2 orders a month at typical conversion. It is the biggest pool left but not a step change on its own; it grows turnover mainly through (a) kits that clear the $300 minimum and (b) cross-sell of notes to buyers who arrive for the gun.
- Competition for the head term is likely big retailers (toy and party stores). KD 28 says it is reachable; the page needs strong images, reviews of the real product and a clear price.
- Time to rank is weeks to months for a new page, so index it early.

## 6. Decisions needed from the owner (blockers)

2. Sell price and kit contents: gun + how many notes, and whether to set a lower minimum for this product.
3. Battery vs mains model, after the Australia Post check.
4. Product photos: supplier images with permission, or your own photos.
