# How to grow keyword volume and turnover — 2026-10-20

Data: Semrush AU, refreshed 2026-10-19/20. Current GSC baseline (28 days to 2026-10-17): 17 clicks, 416 impressions, average position 24.9.

## The honest ceiling
Everything the site sells today, as search demand, is small. The prop-money head terms add up to roughly 2,000 searches a month in Australia
(australian prop money 720, prop money australia 590, au prop money 390, plus a few dozen small variants). Every buying phrase
("buy prop money", "cheap prop money", "can you buy prop money") is 10 to 20 searches a month. Ranking all of them perfectly would still be a
modest number of visitors. To match a high-turnover goal, volume has to come from four places, in this order.

Next step for this lever: links. The denomination pages and the play money category need a handful of relevant links each to move from page 5 to page 1.

These are the only demand the current catalogue cannot serve. Each is an owner decision on stock, not an SEO change.

## Lever 3: lift what each visit is worth (turnover, not traffic)
- **Minimum order.** The competitor .org advertises $150, ours is $300. A $150 to $200 "scene pack" (Starter Stack plus one note denomination) would convert more small buyers. This is a CLAUDE.md rule, so it needs the owner's call.
- **Average order.** Bundle a hero stack with a reserve stack ("scene pack") and show it on the film, photography and event categories.
- **Repeat B2B.** One production company ordering every episode is worth more than a hundred party buyers. The wholesale page, the wholesale guide and a short "trade account" email sequence are the cheapest turnover lever.
- **Trust.** Keep payment options (bank transfer, PayID, crypto 10% off) and the ABN visible near the buy button.

## Lever 4: demand outside Google
- Google Business Profile for the Eltham studio (the "prop shop melbourne" 20/6 and "buy prop money near me" 20/7 terms use the local pack).
- Direct outreach to art departments, prop masters and agencies, using the guides as the pitch.
- Listings on marketplaces where prop buyers already search, with specimen-marked product photos.
- YouTube or short video of the notes on camera (lighting and print quality are searchable topics).

## Lever 5: authority for the terms over KD 28
prop money (480/33), fake money (1,600/31 to 38), imitation money (1,300/38) are held. They need real links and
a stronger site, so they come last. Do not rewrite pages for them yet.
