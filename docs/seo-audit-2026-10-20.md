## 2. Findings, worst first
1. **Thin blog is the biggest problem.** 53 posts average **163 words** (range 130 to 231). The golden rule asks for 1,500+
   with TOC, 5 FAQs and outbound links. Google now sits on 38 "discovered, not indexed" URLs (up from 9), which
   matches a quality signal against the blog. Adding more thin posts makes this worse.
2. **Cross-links:** every post links to /shop (7 to 10 links) but to **no other post** (1 /blog link = the index). No topical
   cluster, so no authority flows between guides.
3. **Cannibalising post pairs** still present: `play-money-vs-prop-money` vs `/shop/event-and-party-props`,
   `prop-money-compliance-checklist` vs `crimes-currency-act...` vs `/compliance` (all "prop money australia laws"),
   `buying-film-props...` vs `/shop/film-and-tv-props`. Duplicate slug `local-vs-overseas-...` appeared in the earlier audit; re-check it.
4. **CTR gap at good positions:** `/compliance/` has 94 impressions at position 6.7 with 0 clicks. The blog post
   `fake-money-vs-prop-money-the-legal-difference` has 44 impressions at 21.8 with 0 clicks. Fix the title and meta on both.
5. **`/compliance` (no slash) gets impressions** (4) and Footer.tsx:154 and Nav.tsx:338 link to the no-slash form, so every
   click takes a 308 hop. Link to `/compliance/`.
6. **Keyword-bank drift:** `imitation money` is now KD 38 (was 25), `fake money` KD 36. Both break the KD<=28 rule and sit
   in secondary sets as supporting terms. Keep them as supporting copy only, never as a primary.
   `australian prop money` fell to **KD 21** (was 30), which makes it the best head-term opportunity on the site.
7. **Homepage HTML is 254 KB** (shop pages ~80 to 98 KB). Check for inlined data; it feeds the LCP 4.0s noted before.
8. Positives: one H1 per page, canonical present on all pages checked, Product/FAQPage/BreadcrumbList schema live, sitemap 82 URLs, titles start
   with the primary.

## 3. Competitor: australianpropmoney.org (the one to beat)
The old .com.au domain is not the target. The .org is.
| | .org | us |
|---|---|---|
| Entry price | $50 note $200, $100 note $250 | Starter stack from $115 |
| Minimum order | **$150** | **$300** |
| Payments | EFT, PayID, crypto, Afterpay | crypto 10% off, other methods per checkout |
| Trust | ABN link, Trustpilot badge, "set tested" | specimen marking, signature on delivery |
| Content | blog, videos, legal guide | 53 thin posts, compliance page |
| Weakness | unsourced "RBA compliant / government clearance" claims; "unmarked" shipping and "fails counterfeit pen" copy | we are the compliant one |

**How to beat it without copying its risks:**
- Own the legal queries with sourced content: a definitive Section 22 page and guide with outbound links to
  legislation.gov.au and the RBA. Never use "counterfeit", "unmarked" or "undetectable" framing, as that is the .org's liability and our brand safety.
- Their $150 minimum is the conversion gap. Our $300 floor is a CLAUDE.md rule, so any change is the owner's call. Flag only.
- Match their trust signals we can substantiate (ABN on site, payment options shown near the buy button).
- Beat them on the pages they win with: they rank the homepage and /shop for "australian prop money". Give our homepage the 720/KD21 term as a
  true head-term page (1,000+ words of useful copy, FAQ, links to every category).
